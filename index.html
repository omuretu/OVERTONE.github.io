04 / THE IMAGE & ITS MEANING

WHY WE USE THE PHOTO “LEAP OF FREEDOM”

The motif “Leap to Freedom” was chosen for Gräber, an event centered on Schranz, a hard techno genre originating in Germany. It connects Germany’s historical background with the ideals of freedom and expression embodied in electronic music.

During the division of Germany, the Berlin Wall restricted freedom of movement, and some people risked their lives attempting to cross it in pursuit of freedom. The “Leap to Freedom” symbolizes this historical struggle for freedom and the determination to overcome imposed restrictions.

Following the fall of the Berlin Wall, Berlin’s techno culture flourished amid broader social transformation. Clubs and dance floors became spaces where people gathered through music, fostering new forms of expression and community. The repetitive beats and immersive nature of techno, combined with Schranz’s intense rhythms and powerful sound, create a physical experience of liberation from the constraints of everyday life.

This event draws a parallel between crossing physical barriers in history and transcending established boundaries and conventions through music. By connecting German history, the openness of techno culture, and the raw energy of Schranz, “Leap to Freedom” serves as a symbol linking the historical pursuit of freedom with the contemporary experience of liberation through music.
